## Executive Summary

I specialize in **operationalizing AI governance, safety, and algorithmic risk management** across the enterprise lifecycle. Bridging the gap between high-level regulatory directives (NIST AI RMF 1.0, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001) and production engineering, I architect deterministic release gates, independent **TEVV (Testing, Evaluation, Verification, and Validation)** frameworks, and automated incident containment protocols for high-stakes enterprise AI systems.

> [!NOTE]
> **Active Focus:** Translating socio-technical AI risks (hallucinations, prompt injection, demographic disparity, data leakage) into quantifiable engineering constraints, verifiable telemetry thresholds, and legally defensible governance audit trails.

## Technical & Governance Competencies

* **Institutional Policy & Gatekeeping:** 4-tier deployment gates, model decommissioning triggers, algorithmic waivers, and foundation model vendor due diligence.
* **Accountability & Oversight Architecture:** 4-tier RACI structures across 18 roles, formal veto authority for Risk & Ethics officers, and HITL / HOTL / HIC operational boundaries.
* **Empirical Bias & Fairness Testing:** Socio-technical proxy testing without PII collection, EEOC 4/5ths Rule disparity analysis ($0.80$ to $1.25$ corridor), and counterfactual evaluations.
* **Deterministic Incident Containment:** Enforcing $<15$-minute containment SLAs for critical AI failure modes, automated template fallbacks, and real-time operational circuit breakers.
* **Grounded Retrieval Systems (RAG):** Bounded-corpus knowledge architectures, semantic guardrails, prompt injection mitigation, and verifiable source attribution.
